15-MINUTE RESET

Ride the urge.
Don't obey it.

An urge is a feeling, not an instruction. Move away from the trigger and give your body time to settle.

15:00
1Change location
Stand up. Leave the room or put your phone across the room.
2Slow your breathing
Inhale gently for 4 counts, exhale for 6. Repeat comfortably.
3Name the need
Ask: am I stressed, sad, lonely, tired, or bored?
4Choose a next action
Walk, drink water, message someone, or return to a small task.

If the urge is still present, that's okay. You can repeat the reset or choose another safe activity. This app is support, not a test.
